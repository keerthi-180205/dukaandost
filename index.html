How it works

Shop owners already use WhatsApp every day. DukaanDost works right inside it.

1

Say it

Send a voice note whenever you give goods on credit or receive a payment.

"Ramesh ne 200 de diya"

2

We record it

DukaanDost understands the note, saves the entry and replies with the updated balance.

3

You get paid

See who owes what, send polite payment reminders with your UPI ID, and get a summary every night.

What DukaanDost does

Built for kirana stores, medical shops, salons, tailors and every small business that gives credit.

🎤

Voice credit book

Record credit and payments by voice in your own language. Several entries in one note.

🔔

Payment reminders

Ready-made reminder messages with the amount and your UPI ID, sent from your own WhatsApp.

🌙

Daily summary

Every night: today's sales, credit given, money collected and total pending.

↩️

Easy to correct

Every entry is shown back to you with an Undo button. Your original voice note is kept as proof.

Your data stays safe

We only keep what is needed to run your credit book, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

  • We will never ask for your UPI PIN, OTP or bank password.
  • We only need your UPI ID to show it in your reminders.
  • Your customers' details are used only for your shop's records.
  • DukaanDost keeps records. It never moves your money.
